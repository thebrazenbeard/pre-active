# Contributing

Contributions are welcome only when the contributor has the legal right to submit them and meets the repository's contribution terms.

No prescribed CLA statement is required in a pull-request description. Material
owned by the repository copyright holder may be contributed without a separate
CLA acknowledgment. For material owned by another contributor, the maintainer
must confirm sufficient rights in a documented record before incorporation.
That record may be an affirmative acceptance of CLA.md or a separate license or
permission. Submission or merger of a pull request does not itself constitute
acceptance of CLA.md or grant rights beyond an applicable license. Do not submit
third-party code, copied material, model output, datasets, or other content
unless you have the right to do so and clearly disclose license or attribution
obligations.

A contribution may be rejected when its provenance, licensing, or ownership cannot be verified. Acceptance of a contribution does not grant the contributor commercial rights in the repository as a whole and does not change the repository license.

Security-sensitive issues should not include secrets, credentials, private keys, tokens, or confidential third-party information.
